# Billing and Expense Policy
**Project:** Beta
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024

## 1. Budget Limits
The maximum monthly budget for Project Beta's cloud infrastructure is strictly capped at $5,000 USD.
Any new service provisioning that exceeds this limit requires explicit written approval from the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).

## 2. Expense Reporting
All cloud storage invoices must be submitted to the finance department by the 5th of each month. Late submissions will result in budget cuts for the following quarter.
